# Requirement / 需求

**English:** Integrate the landmark peaks into the perimeter ridge, replace the flat ridge wall with a strongly varied skyline, and allow the lowest saddles to approach 20 meters.

**中文：** 将地标主峰融入外围山脊，去除平直的山墙轮廓，并让最低鞍部能够下探到约 20 米。

## Summary / 概要

**English:** Align the ridge beneath every landmark summit, form localized mountain shoulders below the pointed peaks, lower the intervening saddles, and let low passes participate in the normal water and vegetation rules.

**中文：** 让山脊在每座地标主峰下方对齐，先形成局部宽山肩再抬升尖峰，同时降低峰间鞍部，并让低山口恢复正常的水系与植被规则。

## User Request / 用户需求

**English:** The peaks and ridge do not feel like one mountain system, the ridge is still too flat, and its lowest portions may descend to 20 meters.

**中文：** 山峰和山脊感觉不是一个整体，山脊整体太平，最低可以低到 20 米。

## Scope / 范围

- **English:** Use every landmark summit as a compact ridge anchor so the ridge centerline passes through the summit center and rises into a localized shoulder below it.
- **中文：** 将每座地标主峰作为局部山脊锚点，使脊线中心穿过峰心，并在峰体下方抬出局部山肩。
- **English:** Replace the former 240–300 meter ridge wall with a varied 20–185 meter base profile while retaining the existing 337.5–450 meter pointed summit elevations.
- **中文：** 将原有约 240–300 米的山墙式脊线改为 20–185 米的起伏基线，同时保留现有 337.5–450 米尖峰高度。
- **English:** Build each pointed summit upward from the shared ridge foundation instead of generating an independent cone from the 14-meter outer base.
- **中文：** 让每座尖峰从共享山脊基础继续向上生长，不再从 14 米外围基面生成独立锥体。
- **English:** Gate mountain water protection by the local ridge surface height so low saddles retain river carving, wet materials, and vegetation exclusion while high mountains remain protected.
- **中文：** 按当前位置的山脊表面高度控制高山水系保护，使低鞍部保留河槽雕刻、湿地材质和植被避让，高山区域继续受到保护。
- **English:** Preserve the central terrain, rolling foothills, landmark footprints, 450-meter runtime ceiling, and existing cross-cell continuity.
- **中文：** 保持中央地形、缓丘、地标山体占地范围、450 米运行时上限和现有跨区块连续性不变。

## Acceptance Criteria / 验收标准

- **English:** Perimeter sampling remains continuous above 18 meters and contains at least one saddle at or below 35 meters, allowing terrain detail around the authored 20-meter target.
- **中文：** 外围采样脊线在所有方向保持 18 米以上的连续性，并至少包含一个不高于 35 米的鞍部，为设定的 20 米目标保留地表细节余量。
- **English:** Excluding the immediate summit angles, the ridge's 90th-to-10th percentile height difference is at least 100 meters.
- **中文：** 排除主峰紧邻角度后，山脊高度的第 90 百分位与第 10 百分位之差至少为 100 米。
- **English:** Landmark summits remain between 337.5 and 450 meters; their middle shoulders stay within 140 meters of the visible ridge center and reach at least 68% of the same-direction crest height.
- **中文：** 地标主峰保持在 337.5–450 米；其中段山肩与同方向可见脊线中心的偏差不超过 140 米，且高度至少达到该方向脊顶的 68%。
- **English:** A low-pass river sample remains carved with wet material and vegetation exclusion, while high-mountain river samples remain uncarved and visually suppressed.
- **中文：** 低山口河流采样继续产生河槽、湿润材质和植被避让，高山河流采样则继续保持不被切削并隐藏水系痕迹。
- **English:** Focused tests, the full test suite, the production build, and a fixed-camera visual check pass without application console errors.
- **中文：** 定向测试、完整测试套件、生产构建和固定机位实景检查全部通过，应用控制台无错误。
