# Reference Whitewater and Incised Channel / 参考图式流动白水与内切河槽

## Requirement / 需求

**English:** Match the supplied rapid-river reference by restoring a brighter, higher-contrast flowing-water response, multi-scale directional surface detail, broken hydraulically motivated whitewater, and a more clearly incised Hero-river bank profile. Preserve the existing river topology, Single Layer Water composition pipeline, lake and waterfall materials, gameplay water logic, and draw-call/resource budgets.

**中文：** 参考用户提供的急流图片，恢复更明亮、更高动态范围的流动水光感、沿流向的多尺度水面细节、由水力信号驱动的破碎白水，以及更明确的 Hero 河近岸内切剖面。保留现有河网拓扑、Single Layer Water 合成流程、湖泊和瀑布材质、玩法水体逻辑与绘制/资源预算。

## Summary / 概要

**English:** Correct the flowing-water shader so Beer-Lambert volume lighting is composited only once, use meter-scale macro, medium, and micro flow domains with two anti-aliased GGX lobes, and replace sparse soft blobs with layered rapid, shallow-water, obstacle-wake, and restrained junction foam. Convert lateral flow coordinates to meters for consistent scale. Narrow and steepen only the active Hero-river bank profile while retaining its wider outer wash corridor, then deterministically rebake the lowland height map.

**中文：** 修正流动水 shader，使 Beer-Lambert 体积光学只合成一次；使用米制宏观、中观、微观流向域和两组抗锯齿 GGX 高光；以急流、浅水、障碍尾迹和克制的交汇泡沫替换稀疏模糊白团。横向流动坐标改为米制以统一尺度。只收窄并加陡 Hero 活动河槽近岸剖面，保留外层宽冲刷走廊，随后确定性重烘低地高度图。

## User Request / 用户需求

**English:** The user compared the current local river with a stronger reference and requested implementation of the detailed approved plan. The reference has substantially higher surface luminance variation, small bright glints, broken whitewater, readable downstream motion, shallow bank transitions, and a visibly incised channel instead of a dark flat strip across a broad gravel apron. During implementation the user rejected the obstacle foam as moving white color blocks and required rock wakes to read as attached, hydraulically gated, broken downstream shear rather than generic foam around every stone.

**中文：** 用户将当前本地河流与更理想的参考图逐项比较，并要求实施已批准的详细方案。参考图具有更高的水面亮度变化、细小明亮闪点、破碎白水、清晰下游运动、浅岸过渡和明显下切河槽，而不是覆盖在宽平砾滩上的暗色水带。实施过程中，用户进一步否定了像移动白色色块的障碍物泡沫，要求石后尾迹表现为附着于障碍、受水力条件控制且沿下游剪切破碎的结构，而不是每块石头周围都套用通用白沫。

## Scope / 范围

- **English:** Keep the existing Base-to-WaterComposite pipeline, RGB Beer-Lambert coefficients, bounded screen-space refraction, physical water `F0`, river centerlines, water widths and levels, Y-shaped confluence meshes, lakes, waterfall curtains, mist, particles, collision, and gameplay sampling. / **中文：** 保留 Base 到 WaterComposite 流程、RGB Beer-Lambert 系数、受限屏幕折射、物理水体 `F0`、河流中心线、水宽水位、Y 形汇流网格、湖泊、瀑布幕、水雾、粒子、碰撞和玩法采样。
- **English:** Composite Single Layer Water volume lighting once; use shore/LOD coverage only for geometry coverage. Add meter-scale macro, medium, and micro flow domains, dual derivative-filtered GGX response, soft HDR highlight compression, and an environment reflection source for flowing water. / **中文：** Single Layer Water 体积光学只合成一次，岸缘/LOD 仅控制几何覆盖。增加米制宏观、中观、微观流向域、双层导数过滤 GGX、HDR 高光软压缩，并让流动水使用环境反射源。
- **English:** Build foam from authored rapids, a restrained shallow/flow-speed support term, downstream obstacle wakes, and junction influence capped at `0.08`. Keep absolute time phase laterally coherent and never multiply it by per-vertex speed. / **中文：** 泡沫由人工急流、克制的浅水/流速支撑、下游障碍尾迹和最高 `0.08` 的交汇权重构成。绝对时间相位必须横向一致，不得乘逐顶点速度。
- **English:** Encode obstacle centers as a high-valued sheltered core, isolate only the lower-valued side shear in the shader, keep the upstream side visually empty, end the analytic tail at `2.25r` so runtime interpolation is zero by `2.6r`, and gate the result by rapid or shallow-fast hydraulics. Fully suppress authored rapid foam inside the obstacle envelope; rock wakes remain aerated and never enter the bright foam core. Disturbed reaches use bounded `0.6m x 8` tessellation so the shelter and tail survive triangle interpolation without adding attributes or draw calls. / **中文：** 将障碍中心编码为高值庇护核心，shader 只提取较低值的两侧剪切带；上游侧视觉上为空，解析尾端在 `2.25r` 结束，使运行时插值在 `2.6r` 前归零，并由急流或浅水高速条件门控。障碍包络内完全压制人工急流底泡；石后尾迹只进入含气层，不进入明亮泡沫核心。含障碍河段使用受预算约束的 `0.6m × 8` 细分，使庇护区和尾端经三角插值后仍成立，且不增加属性或 draw call。
- **English:** Multiply broad foam mass by medium breakup and existing micro flecks to create porous aeration rather than translating solid white islands. Restrict near-white cores to the highest residual fragments and keep the wake color contribution subordinate to the rapid surface. / **中文：** 使用中尺度破碎和既有微小碎斑共同调制大尺度泡沫质量，形成多孔含气水，而不是平移实心白色岛块；近白核心只保留在最高残余碎片中，尾迹颜色贡献低于急流水面本身。
- **English:** Anti-alias the Hero bed material footprint across `1.6m` at both reach banks and Y-confluence polygon boundaries, then preserve that CPU gradient in the terrain shader so the fixed one-meter terrain triangulation cannot produce repeating bright teeth. / **中文：** Hero 河段岸线和 Y 形交汇多边形边界的河床材质足迹均使用 `1.6m` 过渡，并在地形 shader 中保留 CPU 渐变，避免固定一米地形三角网格产生重复亮锯齿。
- **English:** Interpret lateral `flowUv` in world meters for every flowing strip and preserve continuous junction projection without adding geometry attributes. / **中文：** 所有流动水带的横向 `flowUv` 改用世界米制，并保持汇流投影连续，不增加几何属性。
- **English:** Modify only the Hero river's near-bank terrain profile: wet banks at or below `1.1m`, effective gravel slopes at or below `5.5m`, narrower bend/noise expansion, a concave rise to a crest up to `2.6m` above water, and Y-shaped terrain through J1/J2. Keep the wider outer wash concept and existing main-channel depth. / **中文：** 只修改 Hero 河近岸地形剖面：湿岸不超过 `1.1m`、有效砾坡不超过 `5.5m`、收紧弯道/噪声放大、使用凹形坡面抬升至最高水位以上 `2.6m` 的岸顶，并让 J1/J2 地形保持 Y 形。保留外层宽冲刷概念和现有主槽深度。
- **English:** Add no texture, render target, render pass, draw call, geometry attribute, flow-map simulation, FFT/JONSWAP motion, vertex wave displacement, or transparent wet-bank overlay. / **中文：** 不新增纹理、渲染目标、渲染通道、draw call、几何属性、流图模拟、FFT/JONSWAP 运动、顶点波浪位移或透明湿岸覆盖层。

## Acceptance Criteria / 验收标准

- **English:** Single Layer Water has one volume/reflection/specular/foam composition, returns finite non-negative water thickness, retains `F0 = 0.02037`, and contains no legacy depth-alpha dilution. Performance retains its transparent fallback. / **中文：** Single Layer Water 只进行一次体积/反射/高光/泡沫合成，返回有限非负水厚，保留 `F0 = 0.02037`，且不再使用旧深度 alpha 稀释；Performance 保留透明回退。
- **English:** Multi-scale surface and porous foam domains move downstream without `uTime * vFlowSpeed`; rapid whitewater is broken and directional, calm water stays mostly clear, and junctions show no circular pool, closed contour, concentric ring, or cross-river stripe. Runtime barycentric sampling across all nine Hero rocks verifies no visible upstream wake, a bounded shelter core, no tail after `2.6r`, and no cross-river tail cap. / **中文：** 多尺度水面和多孔泡沫域在不使用 `uTime * vFlowSpeed` 的情况下顺流移动；急流白水破碎且有方向，平缓水保持清透，汇流处无圆潭、闭合轮廓、同心圈或横跨河道的条纹。对九块 Hero 河石头进行运行时重心插值采样，验证上游无可见尾迹、庇护核心受限、`2.6r` 后无尾迹且尾端不形成横跨河道的白帽。
