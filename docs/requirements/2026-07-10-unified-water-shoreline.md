# Requirement / 需求

## Summary / 概要

**中文：** 统一河流、湖泊、小湖和瀑布的环境状态、流动、岸线层次与透明排序。

**English:** Unify environment state, flow, shoreline layering, and transparency ordering across rivers, lakes, small lakes, and waterfalls.

## User Request / 用户需求

**中文：** 用户要求水体不再像青色透明贴片，并形成湿岸、沉积、浅水、碎泡沫和深水的可信过渡。

**English:** The user requested water that no longer resembles a cyan transparent sheet and instead forms a believable transition through wet bank, sediment, shallow water, broken foam, and deep water.

## Scope / 范围

**中文：** 保留曲线河道和纵向 UV；共享太阳、雾和色板；降低程序噪声成本；打散岸边泡沫；明确水面、瀑布、泡沫和水雾排序；增强瀑布断续水帘和冲击雾。

**English:** Preserve curved river geometry and longitudinal UVs; share sun, fog, and palette; reduce procedural-noise cost; break up shoreline foam; explicitly order surfaces, waterfalls, foam, and mist; improve discontinuous waterfall sheets and impact mist.

## Acceptance Criteria / 验收标准

**中文：** 所有水体高光方向一致并进入同一雾；河水沿曲线方向流动；岸边泡沫不形成连续白线；小湖不再使用独立太阳；瀑布远景不呈纯青竖条；透明层交汇无明显翻转或黑边。

**English:** All water highlights share one direction and fog model; river motion follows the curve; shoreline foam does not form a continuous white outline; small lakes no longer use a separate sun; distant waterfalls are not solid cyan strips; intersecting transparent layers show no obvious sorting flips or black seams.
