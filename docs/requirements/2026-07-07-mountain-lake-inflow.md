# Mountain Lake Inflow / 山地入湖溪流

## Requirement / 需求

English: Make the lake inflow read as snowmelt running down from the mountain into the lake instead of short disconnected shore marks.

中文：让入湖水流看起来像从山上流下并汇入湖泊的融雪溪流，而不是湖岸附近断续的短白痕。

## Summary / 概要

English: The snowmelt paths are extended to higher terrain, widened slightly, carved more visibly into the ground, and faded only after entering the shallow lake edge.

中文：融雪路径延长到更高的地形，略微加宽，更明显地刻入地面，并仅在进入浅水湖岸后淡出。

## User Request / 用户需求

English: The user showed the current lake inflow and said it does not look like it is flowing down from the mountain.

中文：用户展示当前入湖水流，并指出它完全不像从山上流下来的。

## Scope / 范围

English: Update only the existing snowmelt inflow paths, carving, stream readability, and lake-entry fade behavior in the water system. Do not change the lake shape, outlet stream, waterfall, terrain heightmap asset, vegetation, player, camera, or unrelated pending work.

中文：仅更新现有水系中的融雪入湖路径、地形刻槽、溪流可读性和入湖淡出行为。不修改湖泊形状、出水口溪流、瀑布、地形高度图资产、植被、玩家、相机或无关的待提交改动。

## Acceptance Criteria / 验收标准

English:
- At least one inflow visibly descends from higher mountain terrain into the lake.
- Snowmelt water connects into the lake edge instead of stopping as scattered shore marks.
- The stream fades inside the shallow lake edge without forming a long strip across the lake.
- The outlet stream and waterfall remain unchanged in intent.
- Build verification passes.

中文：
- 至少一条入湖水流能明显从更高的山地地形下行进入湖泊。
- 融雪水流连接到湖岸，而不是停留为零散岸边白痕。
- 溪流在浅水湖岸内淡出，不在湖面下形成长条。
- 出水口溪流和瀑布的意图保持不变。
- 构建验证通过。
